Our story

Built for the Busy City

Steampunk Bulldog Bento Bar began as a small meal station near the central airship docks. Our goal was simple: serve reliable, flavorful food to travelers who never have enough time.

Today, our kitchen serves humans, bulldogs, and anthropomorphic animals from every district. Every meal is prepared with fresh ingredients, efficient techniques, and a little mechanical ingenuity.

Efficient

Our steam-powered kitchen keeps every order moving.

Welcoming

Every customer deserves a comfortable place to recharge.

Fresh

Our menu uses seasonal ingredients from local sky-farms.
